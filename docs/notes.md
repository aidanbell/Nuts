# NOTES/RAW THOUGHTS

## Game Flow

Wake up from Hibernation -> Gather Nuts, build jobsites and buildings -> Prepare for winter -> Hibernate and earn Golden Nuts based on nuts gathered that season

## IDEAS

Ideas are mostly unlocks for the game.

> Currently there are some ideas that boost production, but down the road I'd like for those to be treated as science research (see SCIENCE)

## JOBSITES

Jobless squirrels can find nuts, but it's done randomly and on a timer. For more predictable nuts per second, squirrels can be assigned to jobsites. Jobsites will be upgraded to improve rates, and also to add staffing slots. This is kinda treated as the usual "boost at 5 lvls, 10 lvls, 20 lvls, etc". Jobsites will also have a hard level cap, to encourage moving to other jobsites.
Each era has it's own jobsites, and they outright replace the older sites so there are only ever 4 jobsites available.

> Down the road, we'll have to figure out how to increase population caps on buildings, since having 500 squirrels, but only enough jobsites to employ 100 of them is not going to be good. Ideally jobless squirrels should be a consolation instead of a strategy. That being said, there is an avenue to explore in researching and focusing on jobless squirrels to boost their production

## TOWN TAB

This tab will have various "buildings" that will contribute to the Jobsites and/or the wellbeing of the group. Buildings will always cost refined resources

Production buildings will scale with how many are built through staff. Each level will add a slot for an additional squirrel to work.

### BUILDINGS:

- House: Increases population capacity
- Bonfire:
- Barn/Silo: Allows nuts to survive the winter
- Storage: Allow some refined resources to last the winter

### PRODUCTION:

- Reasearch lab: research science to get boosts
- Bonfire: Attracts squirrels
- Refineries: Nutwood, Smeltery, etc
- Trading Post: Kittens game styled trading post
- Glowing Nut Collectors: Auto collects golden nuts for a %

### AMMENITIES:

- Tavern: Base increase to squirrel productivity
- Town Hall: Gives more insight/stats on production. Better squirrel management
- Library: See stats! (nuts per second, the raw boost that upgrades give, etc)

### PROJECTS:

- These can be like Wonders. They'll maybe give a production boost, but for the most part signify the end of an era, and the readiness to progress. Costs will be extreme to give some gating.

## POPULATION

5 Squirrels will join via story checkpoints. They will get us through the first PREHISTORY ERA and into the WOOD AGE. Once we staff them all and unlock further jobsites, we'll get a quest to build housing for them, and a BONFIRE. We'll use the bonfire to slowly recruit squirrels.
Population will be capped by housing, which at first will not survive hibernation. Permanent housing will only come when we get to more sturdy building materials, only then will housing and therefore squirrels survive the winter.
Later on we can send squirrels on expeditions to go and find more squirrels, and other resources as well

## REFINEMENT

Nuts can be crafted into building materials after unlocking them. First it is done manually (we'll add a x5, x10, all buttons eventually to make it quicker). After getting farther into the age, we'll be able to automatically refine resources, this will come obviously at a time cost, but also be treated as a building that needs to be staffed.
